# 08 — Final Thesis Structure

This document describes the structure of the final bachelor thesis for AdPartners.kz. It reconciles three sources:

- the SDU 2025 LaTeX bachelor-thesis template (`~/Desktop/SDU IS and CS Bachelor Thesis Template 2025 - Latex (Unzipped Files)/`),
- the existing diploma document (`~/Desktop/Diplomaa docs/Innovators diploma1.pdf`, 58 pages), and
- the user's requested 5-chapter outline.

The SDU template uses **four** main chapters (Introduction, Literature Review, Design and Methodology, Results and Discussion) plus Conclusion, References, and Appendices. The user's requested 5-chapter outline (Introduction · Literature Review and Market Analysis · Methodology and System Design · Implementation · Testing, Results, and Discussion) is treated as a content map: the "Implementation" chapter is folded into Chapter 3 (Design and Methodology) as a major `\section{Implementation}`, in line with the template's structure. This is exactly how the SDU template's `chapterB.tex` is organised.
